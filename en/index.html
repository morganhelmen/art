BEHIND THE PAINTINGS

Lisa
Wilberg Helmen

My name is Lisa, I am 36 years old, and I live in Frognes in Hokksund with my fiancé, our children and our dog, Pan.

I have loved art for as long as I can remember. My interest in painting, drawing and creating with different materials has followed me through every stage of life. I enjoy exploring different forms of expression and techniques, and I am always curious about what I can learn and create.

Much of what I know about painting, I learned from my grandmother, whose portrait you can also see in the gallery. She was never afraid to speak her mind, which made her my best, and perhaps most honest, critic. She has meant a great deal to my development as an artist.

I have created several commissioned works and have also had the opportunity to exhibit and sell paintings, including at Ormåsen and during Kunst Rundt Elva 2026.

One of my biggest dreams is to have my own studio and gallery one day. A place where I can create, explore new techniques and share the joy of making art with others through workshops and courses in different art forms.
